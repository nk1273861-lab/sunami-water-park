About Sunami Water Park

Built for the way Ghazipur actually spends a hot afternoon

Sunami Water Park sits on the edge of Ghazipur city, built around one idea — a water park should work for everyone in the group at once. That means real slides for the thrill-seekers, a calm lazy river for the ones who'd rather float, and a shallow kids' zone so the youngest don't get left out.

Every pool is treated and tested through the day, our lifeguard team is on duty at every zone, and changing rooms, lockers and food counters are all inside the park — so once you're in, you're set for the whole day.
